This desktop bundle contains MSYS2 Python, GTK and their native dependencies.
Their copyright/license notices remain in runtime/share/licenses and package
metadata is retained under runtime-package-metadata. bundle-manifest.json records
the exact installed versions, package digests and corresponding source archives.
Distribute the matching gui-sources.zip alongside this installer. Those complete
MSYS2 source packages include upstream code, recipes and patches.

Application modules are installed in app. BUILD-INFO.txt and bundle-manifest.json
identify the exact DNSProxy source revision. DNSProxy's LICENSE is included.
